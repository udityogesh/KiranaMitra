## 🎬 Pre-Recording Setup

1. Open PowerShell and launch the Streamlit frontend:
   ```powershell
   cd D:\kiranamitra
   streamlit run app.py
   ```
2. Arrange your browser window at `http://localhost:8501`.
3. In the sidebar, verify that the **Gnani Vachana AI** credentials are shown.
4. Make sure your microphone is connected and working.
